# Snow Village

A calm Christmas world for small kids, for Meta Quest 3 (WebXR).
three.js r187 classic-script build (made from the official module). No other dependencies, no CDN.

## Controls
- Left stick: walk (slow)
- Right stick: left/right is snap turn, up/down is walk
- Trigger, grip, A/B/X/Y: throw snowballs
- On screen: W A S D walks, mouse looks, click throws

## Things in the village
- Big tree with twinkling lights, star and sparkles, toy train with whistle
- Presents that pop open when you walk up to them
- Snowmen that look at you, wave and giggle; snowballs knock off their hats
- Penguins on the ice rink, bunnies that hop away, reindeer in a pen
- Santa's sleigh in the sky, northern lights, falling snow, footprints
- Gingerbread house, candy canes, chimney smoke
- Jingle Bells music box (turn off with the Music box on the start page)
